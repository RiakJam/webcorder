How Webcorder works

  1. Choose Screen, Local video, or Web video.
  2. Select a camera and optional nature background.
  3. Press Preview, then Start recording. Your video saves to ~/Videos/Webcorder.

Web video loads in the reaction player when the site allows embedding. Sites that block embedding need their own browser tab; Webcorder does not bypass those restrictions.
